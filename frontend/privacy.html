Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · Version 2026-11-17

In short: we collect only what is needed to run a school learning platform: who you are, proof you are enrolled, your progress, and your AI tutor chats. Your school’s staff can see your progress. We do not sell your data and we show no ads. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data.

1. Who is responsible for your data

IsipNayan is operated by IsipNayan, Cavite, Philippines. Your school decides which students and staff use the platform and receives the learning data of its own students. We handle personal data in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act 10173), its Implementing Rules, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). Data Protection Officer / privacy contact: miascowweljohn0308@gmail.com.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and verify your account and keep it secure.
  • To deliver lessons, quizzes, practice and your progress and rankings.
  • To answer your questions through the AI tutor, using your school’s course materials.
  • To let teachers and school administrators monitor and support their students.
  • To prevent abuse, fix problems and improve the Service.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Our basis is your consent (and your parent’s or guardian’s if you are under 18) given when you sign up, together with the legitimate purposes of running the Service for your school. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Who can see your data

  • School administrators of your school: your account details, certificate of enrollment, learning activity. Only they can open your certificate.
  • Teachers of your school: your name, learning activity, quiz results and progress.
  • Other students of your school: only your display name and your standing on the ranking.
  • Service providers that process data for us (below), under their own security and privacy terms.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it or to protect people from serious harm.

Service providers we rely on:

  • AI providers (such as Groq and Google Gemini): receive your tutor questions, attached images, and excerpts of course materials in order to generate answers and to search the materials. Please do not type personal details into the chat.
  • Google (Drive storage for course files; Google Fonts for typefaces).
  • Hosting providers (for example Render for the server and Vercel for the web pages) and a content delivery network (jsDelivr) for some page scripts.

5. Transfers outside the Philippines

These providers run servers in other countries, so your data may be processed abroad, including in the United States. We use providers that apply security safeguards and we limit what we send them to what is needed.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and learning data: while your account is active and for 1 year after you leave your school or ask us to close it.
  • Certificate of enrollment: until your school has reviewed it and for 1 year after, then it is deleted.
  • AI tutor conversations: until you delete them or your account is deleted.
  • Audit and security records: 2 years.

After that we delete the data or remove what identifies you, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

7. How we protect it

We hash passwords, separate each school’s data from the others, limit who can open certificates and records, rate-limit sign-in and sign-up attempts, and use encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk we will act on it and notify you and the NPC as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed; to access your data; to object to or withdraw consent for its processing; to correct it; to ask for it to be erased or blocked; to receive a copy in a usable format; to be indemnified for damages from unlawful processing; and to complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph). If you live elsewhere, such as the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you may have similar rights under your local law and we will honor them. To use a right, email miascowweljohn0308@gmail.com. We may need to confirm who you are first. Parents and guardians can make requests for a child.

Withdrawing consent or asking for deletion may mean we can no longer provide your account.

9. Children and students under 18

The Service is meant for school-age learners and their teachers. If you are under 18, your parent or guardian should know that you are using it and agree to this policy. A parent or guardian can ask to see, correct or delete a child’s data using the contact above. We do not knowingly collect data for marketing or profiling from children.

10. Browser storage and third-party requests

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. We use your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your theme, name and chat view. Clearing site data or logging out removes it. Loading fonts and scripts from Google and jsDelivr lets those companies see your IP address.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new date and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

12. Contact
